Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

This policy explains what Clearview Maps ("the app", "we") collects, why, and what you can do about it. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Email address and name, to create your account, sign you in, and show your name to your team.
  • Precise location, to place pins, record GPS tracks, show where you are on the map, and alert you near warning pins, including while the app is in the background. Your location is saved only as part of the pins, tracks and other items you create. We don't keep a running history of where you go.
  • Content you create: pins, photos, videos, notes, folders, lines, shapes and tracks, plus files you import. These are stored so they sync to your devices and your company's team.
  • Identifiers: an account ID, used to link your content and team membership to you.
  • Usage data: app activity such as who created or changed an item and when, used to sync data and keep your team's records accurate.

How we use it

We use this data only to run the app's features: your account, syncing, team sharing, location alerts and support. We don't use it for advertising or sell it to anyone.

Who can see it

  • Your company's team can see the items shared to that company, based on each person's role.
  • Service providers: data is stored with Supabase, our database and file-storage host, and the maps use Apple Maps. They process data only to provide those services.
  • Files you export, such as KML/KMZ or Google Earth exports, go wherever you choose to send them.
  • We may share data if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Location: you can change location access anytime in iPhone Settings. Some features won't work without it.
  • Deleting items: you can delete your pins, photos and other items in the app.
  • Deleting your account: in the app, go to Settings, then Account, then Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and personal data. Items you shared with your company may stay with that company. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Security and retention

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access is restricted by account and team. We keep data while your account is active and delete it when you delete your account, except where the law requires us to keep it.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
