Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

This policy explains what Clearview Maps ("the app", "we") collects, why, and what you can do about it. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Email address and name, to create your account, sign you in, and show your name to your team.
  • Precise location, to place pins, record GPS tracks, show where you are on the map, and alert you near warning pins, including while the app is in the background. Your location is saved as part of the pins, tracks and other items you create.
  • Live location for your team: when your company turns on location sharing for you, your current position is shared with the people on your job folders (and your company's Owners and Managers) so they can see you on the map, including while the app is in the background. Only your latest position is kept, not a history of where you go.
  • Content you create: pins, photos, videos, notes, folders, lines, shapes and tracks, plus files you import. These are stored so they sync to your devices and your company's team.
  • Identifiers: an account ID, used to link your content and team membership to you.
  • Usage data: app activity such as who created, changed or deleted an item and when, used to sync data and keep your team's records accurate.
  • Crash and bug reports: if the app crashes or freezes, iPhone gives the app a technical report (no photos, pins or messages), which is sent with your app version, iPhone model and iOS version. If you send a bug report from Settings, we receive your message, your name and the same app and device details. Your company's Owners and Managers can read these too.

How we use it

We use this data only to run the app's features: your account, syncing, team sharing, location alerts and support. We don't use it for advertising or sell it to anyone.

Who can see it

  • Your company's team can see the items shared to that company, based on each person's role.
  • Service providers: your account and data are stored with Supabase (database and smaller photo copies), full-size photos and videos are stored privately with Cloudflare, and the maps use Apple Maps. They process data only to provide those services.
  • Files you export, such as KML/KMZ or Google Earth exports, go wherever you choose to send them.
  • We may share data if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Location: you can change location access anytime in iPhone Settings. Some features won't work without it.
  • Deleting items: you can delete pins, photos and other items in the app, based on your role. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted, where they can be restored: pins, lines, shapes, photos and videos for 7 days, folders (with everything deleted inside them) for 30 days. After that they're permanently deleted from our servers, including full-size photos and videos. Your company's Owners and Managers, and Crew Leads, can also delete items for good sooner.
  • Deleting your account: in the app, go to Settings, then Account, then Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and personal data. Items you shared with your company may stay with that company. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Security and retention

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access is restricted by account, team and role. We keep data while your account is active and delete it when you delete your account, except where the law requires us to keep it. Deleted items are permanently removed after their time in Recently Deleted (see above).

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
