Common questions

How do I join my company?
Sign in with your email, tap Join Your Company, and enter the join code from your company's Manager or Owner. You can use the app once they approve you.

Why can't I edit or share some things?
New members start as Partners, with limited access. A Manager or Owner can change your role in Team & Access, or you can request a role from there.

How do I start a new company?
Creating a company needs a company setup code. Contact us at the email above to get one.

Can I hide folders without hiding them for everyone?
Yes. Hiding a folder, pin, line or shape only hides it on your own phone.

How do I get my maps into Google Earth?
Use the Google Earth export button, or export a KML/KMZ file. Pin icons are matched to Google Earth's icons.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to Settings, then Account, then Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and your personal data. If you can't get into the app, email us and we'll delete it for you.
